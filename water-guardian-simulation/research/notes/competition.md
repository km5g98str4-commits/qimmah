## D. Conflicts / cautions

1. Three distinct programmes share the words "تحدي الابتكار" — MEWA University Innovation Challenge, MEWA
   Innovation Challenge for Sustainability (Babson), and MoE University Innovation Challenge for Sustainable
   Development. Search summaries merged them; rules here were separated by source URL.
2. "Future Makers" is a DCO-led open call co-branded with MEWA; it is **not** the university challenge and
   **does** require AI, whereas no AI requirement was found for the university challenge.
3. Current-cycle status and deadline for the MEWA University Innovation Challenge could not be established
   from snippets. The MEWA page shows a 2025-07-20 update date; the only university notice found (SEU) is
   slugged 2025. **Open question: is a 2026 cycle open?**
