## 1. وقف الملك عبدالعزيز للعين العزيزية (King Abdulaziz Endowment for Ain Al-Aziziah)

### What it is / does
- REPORTED — Founded 1367 AH (1947) by King Abdulaziz to bring water from **Wadi Fatimah and Wadi Khulais**
  (east of Jeddah) to **Jeddah** via ~**70 km** of pipeline; water reached Jeddah 1 Muharram 1367; endowed
  as a perpetual waqf "على جدة وسكانها وزوارها وحجيجها". It was Jeddah's sole water source until the
  first desalination plant (1389 AH).
  https://www.spa.gov.sa/1970790 · https://www.spa.gov.sa/2344356 · https://ar.wikipedia.org/wiki/العين_العزيزية_(جدة)
- **Important framing:** it is a **Jeddah** water waqf (Makkah *region*), not a Makkah-city Zamzam/sabeel operator.
  The Grand Mosque's water/Zamzam is run by the General Presidency (الرئاسة العامة لشؤون المسجد الحرام).
- REPORTED — Operations include: digging wells, pumping through pipes, **cisterns/tanks to store and
  sterilise water, engineering workshops, water-analysis laboratories**; collecting water fees from
  subscribers in Jeddah; leasing springs; land-concession fees; rental income from waqf buildings;
  **managing and maintaining "مدن الحجاج" (pilgrim cities — three built by the waqf)**.
  https://web.awqaf.gov.sa/en/endowment-example/endowment-ain-al-azizia · https://www.alriyadh.com/2185304 ·
  https://www.alriyadh.com/1535365 ("ثماني عيون")
- REPORTED — Charitable spend: mosques, cemeteries, sadaqat; water-bottle distribution to cemeteries,
  funeral-washing facilities, Islamic centres and charities in Jeddah; 1,800 cartons of water to a
  charity (بر أبو راكة); Rabigh charity distributes "مياه العين العزيزية".
  https://biraborakh.org.sa/news/... · https://rabighbr.org.sa/توزيع-مياه-العين-العزيزية/
- REPORTED — **Hajj sabeel:** distributes **chilled bottled water to pilgrims at King Abdulaziz
  International Airport** (arrival halls, waiting halls, pilgrim buses). https://www.spa.gov.sa/N2120174
- REPORTED — **Village desalination:** opened a desalination plant at **قرية ضيم** with capacity
  **50,000 L/day**, part of a programme of small desalination plants for villages under Jeddah governorate.
  https://www.spa.gov.sa/2221783
- REPORTED — **Digital "سقيا العين العزيزية"** platform (built by Rowaad): customer app + supplier app +
  driver app + management dashboard for ordering/delivering saqya water, tracking orders, reports and
  analytics → implies a **tanker/bottle delivery logistics** operation. https://blog.rh.net.sa/وقف-الملك-عبدالعزيز/ ·
  https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.rowaad.soqiacustomerss
- REPORTED — Participated in **Saudi Water Week 2026** showcasing "75 years of giving". https://www.spa.gov.sa/N2623962
- REPORTED — Official site sections: مشاريع الوقف القائمة, أعمال الوقف الخيرية, إعلان حدود أراضي الوقف
  (land-boundary declaration ⇒ large land holdings around the spring/pipeline corridor).
  https://azizia.org.sa/projectsd · https://azizia.org.sa/projects · https://azizia.org.sa/pages/61

### Assets (numbers)
- REPORTED — 70 km original pipeline; "خزان كيلو 14" (first reservoir); network of "بازانات"
  (public cisterns) across Jeddah; 8 springs leased (alriyadh 1535365); wells; labs.

## 5. Synthesis — five problem areas most likely to resonate with the two partners

1. **Sabeel / cooler / bottled-water logistics and waste** (waqf core business + Hajj plastic): 30k Zamzam
   coolers refilled 5×/day, 3 M bottles/day, 15 M sabeel bottles, plastic 23 % of waste. Prototype:
   cooler-level/temperature/energy telemetry, refill-route optimisation, refill-instead-of-bottle stations.
2. **Ablution & toilet water reuse at mosques** (800 m³/day ablution at the Haram already has a reuse
   study; 5,400 m³/day toilets): decentralised greywater/ablution recycling unit for Makkah mosques and
   pilgrim cities, with fatwa-compliant non-potable reuse for cleaning and irrigation.
3. **AC condensate & greywater for street-tree irrigation** (7.7 M-tree target; Makkah municipality
   >1 M m of irrigation network; hotels' huge cooling loads; humid Jeddah): condensate harvesting and
   smart drip control — directly serves "أخضر مكة" and the MoU's carbon-from-afforestation item.
4. **Spring/well/pipeline integrity in Wadi Fatimah–Khulais** (waqf's 70 km legacy system; regional dam
   programme): low-cost leak/flow/quality sensing, tank-level telemetry, groundwater monitoring.
5. **Taif highlands agri-water** (roses 700 ha/1,300 farms, honey 300 t, abandoned terraces & bee villages
   from water scarcity): rainwater-harvesting/terrace irrigation controllers, hive microclimate/water for bees.

Secondary: air-quality/heat sensing for pilgrims (MoU item 3; Arafat misting project), flood-wadi
check-dams that boost vegetation.
