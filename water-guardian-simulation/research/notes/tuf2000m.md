## 1. Pipe diameter range per transducer — SECONDARY / CONFLICT

P1 does **not** give transducer ranges. It only lists M23 transducer-type codes (p.13–14):
`16. Clamp-on TM-1 (Middle size…)`, `18. Calmp-on TS-1 (small size…)`, `20. Clamp-on TL-1`,
`17. Insertion TC-1`, `21. Insertion TLC-2`, plus `10. Standard-M1`, `11. Standard-S1`, `12. Standard-L1`.
Note: **TS-2 does not appear by name in the v13.44 M23 list** — vendors ship TS-2 with the meter set to
"Standard-S1"/"TS-1"-class entry; C3 says "Transducer type register belum ditemukan alamat yang valid" (register for it not found).

Vendor datasheets (search snippets from Amazon listings B0D4PS36M2, B0CN2YQ4SB, B07SGWR1LM, B0FP5132YH; TUF-2000F listing B07RZXY9DM; ecefast datasheet index):

|---|---|---|---|

## 2. Pipe materials — VERIFIED (P1 p.13)

M14 verbatim: "Standard pipe materials (no need to enter material sound speed) include:
(0) carbon steel (1) stainless steel (2) cast iron (3) ductile iron (4) copper (5) PVC (6) aluminum, (7) asbestos (8) fiberglass (9) other( need to enter material sound speed in M15)".

- PVC: listed (5). Copper: listed (4). Carbon steel / stainless: listed (0)/(1). Galvanized steel: **not listed by name** (use carbon steel).
- **CPVC: NOT listed.** **PPR / PP-R: NOT listed as a pipe material.** Polypropylene appears only as a *liner* material: M16 "(4) Polypropylene" (p.13). For a PPR pipe you must select M14 = 9 "other" and enter the material sound speed in M15 (value NOT FOUND in any TUF document; must come from a generic sound-speed table — not researched here).
- Vendor listings say "Used for Metal Or PVC Plastic Pipe" (SECONDARY).

## 3. Velocity range, accuracy, repeatability, linearity

- Accuracy / repeatability — VERIFIED P1 p.6 §2.2: "1. better than 1% accuracy, better than 0.2% repeatability." Current-loop output accuracy "better than 0.1%" (p.31).
- Linearity — SECONDARY: "linearity 0.5%" (TUF-2000F/B/H listings & ecefast datasheet snippet). Not in P1.
- Velocity range — **CONFLICT / not in P1**. P1 only says (p.6): "It can measure a flow that is as low as 0.001m/S properly when the PI-transducers are used." (PI = pipe-section transducers, not clamp-on.)
  Secondary: "±0.01 m/s to ±12 m/s" (TUF-2000B manual/datasheet snippets, several listings); "0~±10 m/s" (Amazon TS-2 listing B0D4PS36M2); "0.01 m/s to 32 m/s" (TSONIC TUF-2000 series manual on manualslib, snippet). Treat ±0.01–±12 m/s as the common vendor claim; 32 m/s is an outlier.
- Default low-flow cutoff is 0.03 m/s (P1 p.30) — i.e. the firmware itself zeroes anything below 0.03 m/s unless you lower M41.

## 4. Straight pipe requirements — VERIFIED (P1 p.23–24, table text is garbled by extraction)

Principles (p.24): "Install the transducers on a longer length of the straight pipe. The longer the better, and make sure that the pipe is completely full of liquid."
Table values recovered from p.23 (figure captions "Upstream Dimension L up x Diameters / Downstream Dimension L dn x Diameters"): upstream **10D** (single elbow), **10D**, **12D**, **20D**, **20D** (worse fittings, e.g. double elbows / reducers / valves), **30D** downstream of a pump; downstream always **5D**. Exact fitting-to-value mapping cannot be read from the extracted text; the standard TUF-2000S datasheet (snippet) states "upstream 10D, downstream 5D, and 30D from the pump". C5 (auraflow) uses "≥ 10× pipe OD upstream, ≥ 5× downstream".

## 5. Temperature limits — SECONDARY (see §1 table). Not in P1. Std clamp-on −30~90 °C; HT −30~160 °C; insertion TC-1 −40~160 °C. P1 fluid list includes "(15) Hot water at 125C" (M20, p.13).

## 6. Power supply & consumption — VERIFIED (P1)

- p.4 §1.5: "The applied voltage should be in the range of 8V to 36VDC, or 10V to 30VAC. Avoid applying an 110VAC or 220VAC power forever, or the module will be damaged."
- p.5: "The power supply is 8~36VDC@50Ma (10~36VAC is also applicable)." p.7 §2.2.23: "Power supply by 8VDC~36VDC/50mA or 10VAC~30VAC/50mA." (P1 is internally inconsistent 30 vs 36 VAC.)
- **"10–36 VDC": not what P1 says (it says 8–36 VDC). "85–264 VAC": NOT FOUND for TUF-2000M — the module must not see mains.** (Wall-mount B/S/F variants have mains PSUs, unverified here.)
- Consumption: 50 mA at supply (P1). ≈1.2 W @24 V. TUF-2000B listing says "1.5W" (SECONDARY). C7 measured "~167 mA (approximately 2 W at 12–24 V)" for meter + ESP32 (SECONDARY).
- Backup battery for calendar/totalizers: "When the battery voltage is less than 2 volts, a new backup battery should be replaced" (p.4).

## 7. Outputs — VERIFIED (P1)

- Terminals (p.7–8): `8~36V+/−`, `485+/485−` ("one isolated and surge protected RS485"), `AO+/AO−` "Analog output, loop powered" (p.5 says "isolated 4~40mA current output" — typo for 4–20), `OCT+/OCT−` (M78), `OCT2+/OCT2−` ("RELAY output setup", M79 — "the name RELAY was used other than OCT2, but in fact it is an OCT output"), `AI3/AI4/AI5` analog inputs, `T1/T2/TX1/TX2` PT100.
- OCT ratings (p.18): open collector, "maximum current shall not be over 100mA… maximum voltage applied to OCT can not be over 80 volts." OCT2/"RELAY" (p.18): "SPST… rated for 110VAC max… 0.5A resistive load" (but see note above that it is really an OCT).
- 4–20 mA modes (M55, p.16): 4–20, 0–20, serial-controlled, sound-speed, 20-4-20, 0-4-20, 20-0-20, velocity, heat rate. Ranges set in M56/M57.
- Frequency output (M67–M69, p.17): "Valid range is 0Hz-9999Hz. Factory default value is 0-1000 Hz. For Version-12, Version-13, Version-14 flow meters, you need a hardware module, which shall be plugged to the Serial Expanding Bus, for the frequency output function." Output goes through the OCT (M78).
- Totalizer pulse (p.32–33): "The totalizer output will produce a pulse output with every unit flow of the totalizer" — mapped to OCT (M78 item 9/10/11 = POS/NEG/NET INT Pulse) or buzzer. Resolution = totalizer unit (M32) × multiplier (M33, "0.001 to 10000", default 1). Pulse width M54: "Minimum is 6 mS, maximum is 1000 mS". Limit: "the output device can output only one pulse in a measurement period (500milliseconds)" (p.29) → max 2 pulses/s; choose multiplier so a pulse comes "in several seconds or minutes".
- Register 62 "Pulses left for OCT" lets you see the pulse backlog.

## 8. Modbus register map — VERIFIED (P1 p.39–47) + code cross-checks

Protocol facts (P1 p.39–40, verbatim):
- "A software switch located at the window number 63… select MODBUS-ASCII or MODBUS-RTU… **The default option is MODBUS-ASCII format.**" (p.4: "Select option 1 at Menu 63 for the MOSBUS RTU application.")
- "TUF-2000M can only support MODBUS functions code 3 and code 6, i.e. reading registers and writing a register."
- "By default, the RS232/RS485 will setup with 9600,none,8,1". M62: baud 300–19200, parity none/even/odd, 8 data bits, 1 (or 2) stop bits.
- Address: M46 "Networks address identification number. Any integer can be entered except 13… 10… 42… 38, 65535." "all the parameters of the flow meter can be programmed through the network, except the device address that needs the keypad." Reg 1442 "Device address" (read). C3 tested reg 1442 = 1. Factory default address 1 (C1, C3, HA snippets — SECONDARY; P1 does not state the default).
- Example frame: `01 03 00 00 00 0A C5 CD` reads "REG0001 to REG0010" → **manual register N = PDU address N−1** (1-based table). C3: "Manual uses 1-based addressing… modbus_addr = register_number - 1".
- Data types (P1 note on p.40): "Long is a signed 4-byte integer, lower byte first"; "REAL4 is a format of Singular IEEE-754 number, also called FLOAT"; BCD; BIT; INTEGER (16-bit).

**Word order — CONFLICT in wording, but consistent in practice:** three independent implementations agree that for a 2-register REAL4/LONG at N,N+1 the **first register (N) is the LOW 16-bit word and N+1 is the HIGH word** (i.e. "CDAB"/word-swapped relative to big-endian ABCD):
- C1: `pack("HH", reg1, reg2)` then `unpack("f")` (native little-endian) → reg1 = low word. LONG: `(reg2<<16)|reg1`… actually C1 codes `readLongReg(regOne, regTwo)` as `(regTwo & 0xFFFF) + (regOne << 16)` — **note C1 treats LONG opposite to its FLOAT** (regOne high). Unverified which is right for LONG; C2 treats LONG same as FLOAT (reg N low).
- C2: `ConvertToFloat(LowWord=reg[1], HighWord=reg[2])`.
- C3: worked example "Temperature 36.625°C, Raw bytes: 80 6B 42 12, Swap to: 6B 80 12 42" → reg33 = 0x806B (low word), reg34 = 0x4212 (high word); 0x4212806B = 36.6253 ✓. C3 calls this "BADC" but the effective rule is the same.
- partofthething (snippet): "the data comes in Big Endian" then hand-assembles the float after swapping — wording contradicts the above; page unreadable here. ESPHome users (HA thread 459931, snippet) report `value_type: FP32` at `address: 0x1` — this cannot be reconciled without reading the thread (FP32 = high-word-first in ESPHome; `FP32_R` is the swapped form). **Recommendation: implement as low-word-first (CDAB) and confirm against the LCD on first power-up; C5 exposes a runtime toggle for exactly this reason.**

## 9. Signal diagnostics (M90/M91) — VERIFIED (P1)

- Display-07 on-board (p.11): "SS is the signal strength range in 00~99, normal 50~99… Q is the signal quality range in 0~9, normal 5~9".
- M90 (p.20): "Signal strength is presented by 00.0 to 99.9… Q value is presented by 00 to 99, the bigger the better. It should at least be great than 50 for normal operations."
- Setup checklist (p.22–23): "Generally the signal strength should be better than 60.0, and signal quality should be better than 50.0." §5.4.1: "the instrument works well if the signal strength ranges from 50.0 to 99.9… Relocate… if the signal strength is lower than 60.0." §5.4.2: "Under normal pipe condition, the Q value is in the range 60.0-90.0, the higher the better."
- **CONFLICT (minor):** the Amazon-hosted TUF-2000M manual snippet says "The flow meter can work properly when Q > 60"; v13.44 says >50. Use Q ≥ 60 as the engineering target. libe.net (snippet): "A signal quality of more than 85% is necessary to get stable values" (field experience, SECONDARY).
- M91 (p.20): "the ratio value should be in the range of 100±3%. Otherwise the entered parameters and the transducer installation should be checked." Same in §5.4.3 and reg 97–98 "Normal 100+-3%".
- M92 estimated fluid sound speed (compare to ~1480 m/s for water); M93 total transit time & delta time; M94 Reynolds & pipe factor.
- Reg 93/94 raw strengths are 0–2047, not the 0–99.9 M90 scale; the scaling is NOT FOUND.

## 10. Damping / cutoff / zero — VERIFIED (P1)

- M40 damper (p.15, p.30): "ranges form 0 to 999 seconds. 0 means there is no damping. Factory default is 10 seconds." "Numbers 0 to 30 are commonly used."
- M41 low-flow cutoff (p.30): "The flow meter will replace these flow rate values that are absolutely less than the low-cutoff value with '0'… Generally the default value is 0.03m/s." Note the unit is **velocity (m/s)**, not m³/h.
- M42 zero point (p.4, p.30–31): "Zero setup should always been performed under the condition of motionless liquids"; zero lives in RAM until you run M26 "make default paras" — otherwise it is lost at next power-on. M43 clears/restores the solidified zero; M.2 solidifies the zero (password). p.56: if flow reads 0 while water is running, a bad M42 was done — use M43.
- M44 flow bias (should be 0); M45 scale factor (default 1); M48 linearity correction (default 0 = off, "Try to make sure that value is 0 so as to avoid abnormal flow").
- M28 "hold last good value when poor signal" (default YES) — **beware: a dropout will freeze the last flow value rather than read 0.**
- M29 empty-pipe signal threshold; M.5 Q-threshold: "If the present Q is below this threshold, flow rate will be set to 0… useful… on airy pipes."
- M26 must be run after any setting change or the meter reverts at power cycle (p.4 §1.2).

## 11. Couplant, mounting, full pipe, bubbles — VERIFIED (P1 p.24–26)

- "Apply adequate coupler to the spot where the transducers are to be installed and leave no gap… Extra care should be taken to avoid any sand or dust particles."
- "To avoid gas bubbles inside the upper part of the pipe, the transducers should be installed horizontally by the side of the pipe."
- "make sure that the pipe is completely full of liquid"; "polishing the pipe with a sander is strongly recommended".
- Methods (M24): V (0) "most widely… inner diameters ranging from 15 mm to 200 mm… also called reflective mode"; Z (1) ">200mm"; N (2) "Rarely used"; W (3) "15mm to 50mm" (four traverses — longer path, more sensitivity on small pipes, but weaker signal).
- Spacing: M25 "refers to the distance of inner spacing between the two transducers"; time ratio (M91) must be 100±3.
- Couplant type not specified in P1; libe.net used Vaseline successfully (snippet); C7 says silicone couplant "degrades… plan regular re-greasing (weeks to months outdoors)".

## 13. Update rate — VERIFIED (P1)

"the measuring period is 500mS" (p.19 M81 note) and "one pulse in a measurement period (500milliseconds)" (p.29). "128 times of sample of original data" per measurement (p.5). Displayed/registered flow is then filtered by M40 damping (default 10 s). Power-on log records "Flow rate after 60 seconds when power on" (p.47) — treat the first minute after boot as settling.

## 14. Real-world reports on small pipes / low flow / zero drift — SECONDARY (search snippets; pages blocked)

- partofthething.com (ESP8266, ~28 mm copper, TS-2): "the TUF-2000M does not appear to be suitable for relatively small copper pipes"; "huge oscillations around zero when there is no flow… in both positive and minus directions"; "the flow rate appears to be steadily… drifting" during operation; "a bit too noisy to detect really low flows… good for detecting and quantifying continuous large flows, like a flushing toilet or running faucet"; adjusted the zero-cutoff (M41) to suppress noise; needed "sensors… separated nearly twice as far apart as called for" on 3/4" copper before getting a usable Q.
- HA thread 734213: user with TS-2 on 22 mm pipe "had trouble getting a good quality reading"; copper wall-thickness tolerance affects spacing.
- libe.net field report (TUF-2000M): needs Q > 85 % for stable values; bucket test showed the meter read ~1.3× too low, corrected with M45 scale factor; M91 near 100 % as install check.
- Reef2Reef (TUF-2000B + TS-2 on 1" Sch40 PVC): installed successfully; "No Signal Detected" until pipe parameters entered correctly. No low-flow numbers given.
- C5 auraflow (ESP32, residential): expects Q ≥ 60, 5-gallon bucket test "within ±5%", "pipe OD/wall values… the single biggest source of accuracy error".
- C7 dossier: "expect 15-30 minutes of adjusting transducer spacing, re-greasing and re-clamping"; "a 1 mm wall thickness error produces confidently incorrect readings"; "requires seasonal zero-point re-calibration with flow stopped".
- No report found of TUF-2000M specifically on **PPR** pipe (NOT FOUND). No quantified minimum detectable flow on DN20–32 found; the firmware floor is M41 = 0.03 m/s default (≈ 0.03 m/s × π(0.010 m)² ≈ 0.034 L/min on DN20 ID 20 mm… i.e. ~2 L/h — but the noise floor reported by users is far above this).

**Engineering takeaway (inference):** on DN20–DN32 plastic, expect zero-flow noise of the order of ±(0.05–0.2) m/s unless M41/M.5/M40 are raised, which in turn hides genuine drips. Do a proper M42 zero with the line shut and solidify via M26; verify M91 = 97–103; poll reg 92 (Q) alongside reg 1 and reject samples with Q < 60 or reg 72 ≠ 0.
