## 2. Tracks / challenge statements (exact wording found)

VERIFIED (snippet, EN official page):

> "The challenge is aimed at encouraging university students in the Kingdom to develop innovative solutions
> addressing specific challenges across three sectors: **Environment (vegetation cover and afforestation)**,
> **Water (water scarcity)**, and **Agriculture (food waste)**."

> "The challenge is structured into distinct elements, each focusing on a key area: **desertification**,
> **water loss and grey water management**, and the **RPW control value chain**."

Sector blurbs (VERIFIED snippet):
- Environment: "Vegetation is a fundamental element in maintaining environmental balance, as it helps reduce
  desertification, improve air quality, and promote sustainability, while afforestation efforts are among the most
  effective solutions to support the environment by increasing green spaces and protecting natural resources."
- Water: "Water scarcity is one of the most pressing environmental challenges, requiring improved efficiency in
  water use and the adoption of sustainable solutions to conserve resources" … "through rationalization of
  consumption, developing alternative sources, and supporting modern technologies."
- Agriculture: only the label "food waste" surfaced; RPW = red palm weevil (سوسة النخيل الحمراء) "control value chain".

Application page variant (VERIFIED snippet, `Application.aspx`): sectors listed as
"**Environment / Vegetation Cover & Afforestation – Water / Water Production – Agriculture / Food Waste**".
→ Conflict to note: overview says "Water (water scarcity)" + "water loss and grey water management";
application page says "Water / Water Production". Both are official; capture exact wording on the live page.

AR wording: NOT FOUND verbatim (AR page not quoted by engines). AR press paraphrase: «الأمن الغذائي والمائي
وإعادة التشجير».

**Fit note for a water-loss / leak-monitoring project:** "water loss" is an explicit named element of the water
track. Whether it means utility NRW, on-site (building/mosque) losses, or irrigation losses is **not specified**.

Pattern inference (weak, from track design only): MEWA frames each track as a *value chain / operational problem*
(RPW control value chain, water loss + grey-water reuse, afforestation/desertification), and the reward is a
co-run **pilot** — so proposals that name a deployable pilot site and a measurable KPI (m³ saved, % NRW cut,
survival rate of seedlings, infestation detection rate) match the framing better than pure research.
