About Qimmah

Qimmah is an Arabic-first fitness app: a clear training plan, meal logging with barcode scan, and honest progress tracking — your whole day in one place. Local-first, no ads, no hype.

Full description

Qimmah brings training, nutrition, measurements, and progress into one Arabic-first system. It builds a plan around your goal (cut / maintain / bulk), logs meals and calories, and reads your week in honest, hedged language — never a verdict. Local-first: your data stays on device; cloud sync is optional on sign-in. No ads, no cross-app tracking.

Quick facts
