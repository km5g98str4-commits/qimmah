Who We Are
Founded in 1988 by a group of visionary young Saudis, KUDU has significantly redefined the dining landscape in Saudi Arabia. Our journey began with the opening of the first restaurant in Riyadh on April 16, 1988, where we introduced a fresh concept of made-to-order sandwiches, within a live theatrical open kitchen environment.
Learn More
Our Journey
Since opening our doors in 1988, KUDU has grown from a pioneering establishment into a beloved cornerstone of Saudi Arabia's dining culture. Over the past 35 years, we've expanded to 350 locations, each a celebration of the vibrant spirit and rich traditions that define our nation.
