don’t worry, eat out happy

We understand that everyone’s eating habits are different, which is why we’re proud to work in a scratch kitchen that
affords us to cook for everyone. When you have a food allergy or dietary restriction, eating out can be stressful.

At P.F. Chang’s, we want you to be able to celebrate the food on your plate and the people you’re with.

GLUTEN-FREE MENU

We have had a separate gluten-free menu for nearly a decade. Those with a gluten allergy or intolerance can order P.F. Chang’s favorites like Chang’s Lettuce Wraps and Chang’s Spicy Chicken knowing their food will be made completely free of gluten.

Our chefs use a designated gluten-free cooking area with gluten-free woks and cooking utensils. Many of our restaurants use designated gluten-free plates as an extra step to ensure your meal is gluten free.

Always alert your server when ordering gluten free.

View GF Menu

ALLERGY INFORMATION

Being in the food business, we are aware that many people have serious food allergies. Peanuts is a big one for us because it’s an ingredient used a lot in Asian sauces. Luckily, we’ve thought this through. Every kitchen in every restaurant is equipped with an extensive allergy matrix.

It’s very important that you let your server know about any food allergies upon ordering. This will ensure that the proper steps are followed so you can safely enjoy your meal.
